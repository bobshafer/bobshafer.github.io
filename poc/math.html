PoC Bookkeeping Model (Mathematical Core)

0. Base structure: an enriched category of continuations

Let \(\mathbf C\) be a (small) category.

  • Objects \(X,Y,\dots\in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathbf C)\) are “systems / states at a chosen resolution.”
  • Morphisms \(f:X\to Y\) are admissible continuations.

Each morphism has a viability \[ v(f)\in(0,1] \] interpreted as fuzzy truth / graded admissibility.

If a continuation is impossible, then no morphism exists; equivalently, \(v=0\) is treated as “undefined morphism”.

Identity viability: \[ v(\mathrm{id}_X)=1 \]

Composition viability (serial continuation = fuzzy AND): \[ v(g\circ f)=v(g)\,v(f) \]

This makes \(\mathbf C\) a category enriched over the quantale \(([0,1],\le,\sup,\cdot)\), i.e. the \((\max,\times)\) semiring.

1. Lawvere cost enrichment

Define the PoC cost functional: \[ D(f):=-\ln(v(f))\in[0,\infty) \]

Then:

  • \(D(\mathrm{id}_X)=0\)
  • \(D(g\circ f)=D(g)+D(f)\)

So \(\mathbf C\) is equivalently enriched over the Lawvere quantale \(([0,\infty],\ge,\inf,+)\).

2. Global reachability viability (parallel continuation = fuzzy OR)

For objects \(X,Y\), define the viability of reaching \(Y\) from \(X\):

\[ V(X\Rightarrow Y):=\sup_{\gamma\in \Gamma(X,Y)} v(\gamma) \]

where \(\Gamma(X,Y)\) is the set of all composable morphism chains \(\gamma:X\to Y\).

Equivalently, in cost form:

\[ d(X,Y):=\inf_{\gamma\in\Gamma(X,Y)} D(\gamma) \]

Then:

\[ V(X\Rightarrow Y)=e^{-d(X,Y)} \]

So \(d\) is the induced Lawvere metric on objects.

This is the first “spacetime-like” bookkeeping object: distance = minimal continuation cost.

3. Spheres of becoming (viability balls)

Define the sphere (ball) of becoming of radius \(r\) around \(X\):

\[ B_r(X):=\{Y\in\mathrm{Ob}(\mathbf C)\mid d(X,Y)\le r\} \]

Equivalently, define it by a viability threshold \(\alpha\in(0,1]\):

\[ B_\alpha(X):=\{Y\mid V(X\Rightarrow Y)\ge \alpha\} \]

with \(\alpha=e^{-r}\).

So “future cones” are literally iso-cost / iso-viability contours.

4. Continuation functor (the presheaf of viable futures)

Define the continuation presheaf:

\[ \mathcal V:\mathbf C^{op}\to \mathbf{Set} \]

where \[ \mathcal V(X)=\{(f:X\to Y)\} \] or, more strongly, the set of future objects weighted by \(V(X\Rightarrow Y)\).

The key PoC constraint is:

\[ \mathcal V(X)\neq\varnothing \] (continuation exists)

and also a bounded “navigability” constraint, e.g. finite entropy or finite effective branching.

5. Elastic regime: amplitude refinement

In the elastic regime, each morphism carries a complex amplitude:

\[ \psi(f)=\sqrt{v(f)}\,e^{i\theta(f)} \]

so that \[ |\psi(f)|^2=v(f) \]

Composition:

\[ \psi(g\circ f)=\psi(g)\psi(f) \]

Thus:

  • viability is amplitude magnitude squared
  • phase is additive: \(\theta(g\circ f)=\theta(g)+\theta(f)\)

For a set of alternative morphism chains \(\gamma_i\) from \(X\) to \(Y\), define total amplitude:

\[ \mathcal A(X\Rightarrow Y)=\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma(X,Y)} \psi(\gamma) \]

and probability of outcome \(Y\):

\[ P(Y|X)=\frac{|\mathcal A(X\Rightarrow Y)|^2}{\sum_{Y'}|\mathcal A(X\Rightarrow Y')|^2} \]

This is exactly “interference of continuations.”

6. Plastic regime: pruning as a natural transformation

A record-writing event corresponds to a restriction of admissible futures:

\[ \mathcal V \Rightarrow \mathcal V' \]

with \[ \mathcal V'(X)\subseteq \mathcal V(X) \]

Equivalently, pruning deletes morphisms (or assigns them viability 0 / infinite cost).

This is the categorical collapse: contraction of the sphere of becoming.

7. History: colimit of stabilization

Let \(T\) be an index category (often a poset).

Evolution is a diagram:

\[ D:T\to \mathbf C \]

The accumulated history is its colimit:

\[ H=\mathrm{colim}(D) \]

Irreversibility is the non-invertibility of the colimit gluing.

This is the formal “time arrow.”

8. KIUAN: regime change as category extension

A KIUAN is not a choice of an element of \(\mathcal V(X)\).

It is a change of the base category:

\[ i:\mathbf C \hookrightarrow \mathbf C' \]

such that new objects/morphisms become admissible and restore viability:

\[ \mathcal V'(iX)\neq\varnothing \]

This is “new degrees of freedom.”

9. Spacetime + fields as derived bookkeeping

A spacetime chart is a projection:

\[ \pi:\mathrm{Ob}(\mathbf C)\to \Omega \]

such that:

  • neighborhoods in \(\Omega\) correspond to low-cost balls \(B_r(X)\)
  • locality is induced by restriction maps of a Grothendieck topology on contexts

Fields are sheaves over \(\Omega\), i.e. section-valued bookkeeping of local continuation state.

One-line summary of the bookkeeping model

PoC bookkeeping is an enriched category of continuations where viability is fuzzy truth, serial continuation multiplies viabilities, alternative continuation takes sup (or amplitude-sum in the elastic regime), and spacetime emerges as the coarse-grained Lawvere geometry induced by \(-\ln v\).

That is the complete formal skeleton. Everything else (GR/QFT correspondence, gauge structure, renormalization) is downstream specialization.
