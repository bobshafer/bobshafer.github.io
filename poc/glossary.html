PoC Glossary

Reference glossary for terms used across Introduction, Math Formalism, Reasons to Care, The Lie We Tell, To Mind, To Matter, and To Interfere.

Core Terms

Principle of Continuation (PoC)
The thesis that existence is persistence of viable continuation, not static being.

Continuation
An admissible next step from one state/system to another.

State
A system description at a chosen resolution.

Admissibility
Whether a continuation is allowed under current constraints.

Viability
A graded admissibility value \(v \in (0,1]\). Higher means more coherent/maintainable continuation.

Fuzzy Truth
The interpretation of viability as a degree of truth-like admissibility, not a probability.

Continuation Cost
The cost transform \(D(f):=-\ln(v(f))\), where higher cost means lower viability.

Lawvere Metric / Lawvere Distance
The induced distance \(d(X,Y)\) given by minimal continuation cost over paths from \(X\) to \(Y\).

Reachability Viability
The best viable reach from \(X\) to \(Y\): \(V(X\Rightarrow Y)\).

Sphere of Becoming
A viability/cost neighborhood of reachable futures, e.g. \(B_r(X)\).

Future Cone (PoC sense)
The set of futures still viable from a current state, represented by viability balls/spheres.

Bookkeeping Model
The formal accounting framework for viability, cost, pruning, stabilization, and extension.

O-Mode
The elastic/open regime in which multiple continuations remain live.

G-Mode
The committed/inscribed regime in which a continuation has become record.

Category/Formalism Terms

Category \(\mathbf{C}\)
Objects are states/systems; morphisms are admissible continuations.

Object
A node/state in the continuation category.

Morphism
A continuation map \(f:X\to Y\) between objects.

Identity Morphism
\(\mathrm{id}_X\), the zero-cost self-continuation at \(X\).

Composition
Serial continuation \(g\circ f\), combining steps along a path.

Serial AND Rule
In viability form: \(v(g\circ f)=v(g)\,v(f)\).

Parallel OR Rule
Alternative chains combine by supremum in viability mode: \(V=\sup v(\gamma)\).

Branching Structure
Formal bookkeeping of parallel continuation alternatives before commitment.

Distributivity (Branching/Sequence Compatibility)
Requirement that serial continuation composes consistently with branching alternatives.

Free Linear Completion
The canonical linear completion of path/continuation structure used to derive superposition.

Presheaf of Futures \(\mathcal{V}\)
Bookkeeping object assigning available continuations/futures to each context/state.

Natural Transformation (Pruning Map)
A structure-preserving map \(\mathcal{V}\Rightarrow\mathcal{V}'\) encoding reduction of admissible futures.

Enrichment
Viewing \(\mathbf{C}\) with extra structure (viability semiring or Lawvere-cost quantale).

Colimit (History)
Formal construction for accumulated stabilized structure over a process/diagram.

Boolean Event Algebra
Classical event structure of committed record, supporting ordinary logical operations and additive probability.

Regime and Dynamics Terms

Elastic Regime
Coherent regime where alternatives combine as amplitudes and can interfere.

Committed Regime
Regime of definite record/history where classical Boolean probabilities apply.

Amplitude
Complex continuation weight \(\psi(f)=\sqrt{v(f)}e^{i\theta(f)}\).

Phase
\(\theta(f)\), the angle component of amplitude controlling interference behavior.

Interference of Continuations
Outcome effect from summing amplitudes over alternative continuation chains.

Phase Indifference
Compatibility requirement that commitment probability depends on amplitude magnitude, not overall phase.

Chain Rule Compatibility
Requirement that serial commitment probabilities multiply consistently with serial amplitude multiplicativity.

Born Forcing
The compatibility result that fixes the probability power law exponent to \(2\), yielding the Born rule.

Plastic Regime
Regime where recording/commitment prunes futures and yields effectively irreversible stabilization.

Collapse
Contraction of future possibilities via pruning/recording.

Record
A stabilization event that constrains later futures and contributes to irreversibility.

Irreversibility
Structural non-invertibility after stabilization; not merely subjective time flow.

Stabilization
Any operation that makes continuation more persistent by reducing ambiguity or adding structure.

Pruning (Contraction)
Survival by narrowing options; removes incompatible futures.

Extension (Expansion)
Survival by enlarging the continuation space with new options/degrees of freedom.

KIUAN
“Kick It Up A Notch” (ht: Emeril). In PoC: regime expansion, modeled as category extension.

Compatibility Interface
The constrained bridge between elastic amplitudes and committed classical probabilities.

Emergent-Structure Terms

Spacetime (PoC view)
A derived coarse-grained chart over continuation structure, not primitive substrate.

Field (PoC view)
A local bookkeeping section/sheaf over derived spacetime contexts.

Locality
Constraint structure induced by low-cost neighborhoods and context-restriction maps.

Essay/Interpretive Terms (To Mind, To Interfere)

To Mind (verb)
To sustain continuation by active attention, maintenance, and boundary-keeping.

Mind (noun, PoC sense)
A stable continuation engine that represents futures, evaluates viability, and commits.

Continuation Engine
A system that can model options, select survivable paths, and enact stabilizing commitments.

Ownership of Continuation
Whether a system merely persists passively or actively maintains conditions for its own persistence.

Failure by Scarcity
Collapse from too few viable options.

Failure by Overload
Collapse from too many options/information to navigate coherently.

History Is What Remained
PoC framing: history is stabilized structure that survived and now constrains future continuations.

Interference (essay sense)
Combination of multiple paths/reasons that changes what happens next.

Constructive Interference
Aligned paths that reinforce one another and increase pull toward a future.

Destructive Interference
Conflicting paths that cancel and reduce navigability/decision clarity.

Aligned Reasons
Multiple independent reasons pointing to the same action, producing felt conviction.

Out-of-Phase Reasons
Value/reason conflicts that make decisions feel hard despite available information.

Synergy (PoC framing)
Constructive interference in coordinated systems (e.g., teams), beyond simple addition.

Collapsed Interference
A chosen/stabilized outcome after many possible paths were available.
