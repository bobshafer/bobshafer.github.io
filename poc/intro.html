Principle of Continuation (PoC): Axioms and Bookkeeping Framework

A brief introduction to the mathematical model

The Principle of Continuation (PoC) begins from a minimal thesis:

A system exists only insofar as it has admissible next states.

Existence is not taken as primitive “being,” but as the persistence of a navigable continuation structure. What we normally call “objects,” “laws,” “agents,” and even “identities” are, under PoC, stabilized patterns of viable continuation.

The mathematical model below is a bookkeeping formalism for this idea. Its purpose is not to replace physics or logic, but to supply a shared substrate language in which stability, irreversibility, collapse, agency, and regime shifts can be expressed in a unified way.

A. PoC Axioms (Minimal Charter)

Axiom 1 — Continuation is primitive

There exists a state substrate \(\mathcal{X}\) (not assumed to be spacetime) such that for each state \(x\in\mathcal{X}\) there is a set of admissible next states.

A state without admissible continuation is not a viable “thing.”

Axiom 2 — Admissibility is graded (fuzzy truth)

Continuation is not binary. There exists a viability function:

\[ v \in (0,1] \]

interpreted as a fuzzy truth value: the degree to which a continuation is admissible.

  • \(v=1\) means fully admissible.
  • \(0<v<1\) means admissible but strained / costly / unstable.
  • \(v=0\) is interpreted as impossible (no continuation exists).

Thus viability is not probability; it is graded coherence.

Axiom 3 — Continuations compose

Continuation is compositional. If \(f\) continues \(X\) into \(Y\), and \(g\) continues \(Y\) into \(Z\), then their composition continues \(X\) into \(Z\).

This is the basic reason category theory is the natural language for PoC.

Axiom 4 — Serial continuation multiplies viability

If two continuation steps must both hold, their viability composes multiplicatively:

\[ v(g\circ f)=v(g)\,v(f) \]

This is the product t-norm: serial continuation is fuzzy AND.

Axiom 5 — Alternatives compete by maximum viability

If multiple continuation chains exist, the viability of reaching an outcome is determined by the most viable chain (or, in elastic regimes, by interference—see below).

Thus parallel continuation behaves as fuzzy OR:

\[ v(\text{reach }Y)=\sup_\gamma v(\gamma) \]

Axiom 6 — Stabilization occurs by pruning or expansion

A system stabilizes its continuation in one of two ways:

  1. Commitment (contraction): remove incompatible futures (prune).
  2. Innovation (extension): enlarge the space of admissible continuations (KIUAN).

These are distinct operations: pruning modifies what is allowed inside a regime; KIUAN modifies the regime itself.

KIUAN = “Kick It Up A Notch” (ht: Emeril).

Axiom 7 — Records create irreversibility

Some stabilizations are non-invertible. Once a record is written, the prior continuation space cannot be recovered without additional information that no longer exists.

Irreversibility is therefore structural: it arises from non-isomorphic stabilization morphisms.

Axiom 8 — Elastic regimes admit interference

In sufficiently coherent regimes, continuations do not merely compete by max viability; they combine as amplitudes with phase.

Thus interference is a property of continuation structure itself, not of “particles.”

B. The Bookkeeping Model (Core Construction)

Given these axioms, the bookkeeping formalism proceeds by representing continuation as an enriched category.

1. The continuation category

Let \(\mathbf{C}\) be a category where:

  • objects \(X,Y\) are systems/states at some resolution
  • morphisms \(f:X\to Y\) are admissible continuations

Each morphism carries a viability value:

\[ v(f)\in(0,1] \]

with \(v(\mathrm{id}_X)=1\).

2. The PoC Lawvere cost

Define the continuation cost functional:

\[ D(f):=-\ln(v(f)) \in [0,\infty) \]

So:

  • full viability (\(v=1\)) corresponds to zero cost (\(D=0\))
  • impossibility corresponds to infinite cost

Serial composition becomes additive:

\[ D(g\circ f)=D(g)+D(f) \]

This is the key bookkeeping transform: it makes continuation geometry linear.

3. Induced distance between states

Define the minimal continuation cost from \(X\) to \(Y\):

\[ d(X,Y):=\inf_{\gamma:X\to Y} D(\gamma) \]

This is a Lawvere-style metric on the object space.

Equivalently:

\[ V(X\Rightarrow Y)=e^{-d(X,Y)} \]

So “distance” is simply “how much viability must be spent.”

4. Spheres of becoming

The sphere of becoming of radius \(r\) around \(X\) is:

\[ B_r(X):=\{Y : d(X,Y)\le r\} \]

This is PoC’s future cone expressed geometrically: a boundary of equal continuation cost.

5. Collapse and stabilization

A record-writing event is a pruning operation: it deletes morphisms or lowers their viability.

Formally this is a natural transformation on the continuation presheaf:

\[ \mathcal{V}\Rightarrow \mathcal{V}' \]

with \(\mathcal{V}'(X)\subseteq \mathcal{V}(X)\).

This is “collapse” as sphere contraction.

6. KIUAN (regime expansion)

A KIUAN is not selection inside \(\mathbf{C}\). It is extension of the base category:

\[ \mathbf{C}\hookrightarrow \mathbf{C}' \]

such that previously impossible continuation becomes possible.

7. Phase and interference (elastic regime)

In elastic regimes, each morphism also carries a phase \(\theta(f)\) and thus a complex amplitude:

\[ \psi(f)=\sqrt{v(f)}\,e^{i\theta(f)} \]

Composition multiplies amplitudes:

\[ \psi(g\circ f)=\psi(g)\psi(f) \]

Alternative continuation chains sum:

\[ \mathcal{A}(X\Rightarrow Y)=\sum_{\gamma:X\to Y}\psi(\gamma) \]

and outcomes arise from squared magnitude.

Thus quantum interference is simply the coherent superposition of continuation morphism chains.

C. What this bookkeeping is for

This framework provides a single language for:

  • stability as persistence of viable morphisms,
  • irreversibility as non-invertible colimit gluing,
  • collapse as natural-transformation pruning,
  • agency as endofunctors preserving viability,
  • geometry as the large-scale shape of continuation cost,
  • and fields/spacetime as derived coordinate charts on this continuation geometry.

In short: PoC does not begin with “objects moving through time.” It begins with “continuation as a structured viability geometry,” and derives the familiar pictures as stabilized projections.
